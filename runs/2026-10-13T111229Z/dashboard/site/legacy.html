Open-Science Uptake Across Stroke and Adjacent Fields

This benchmark covers 1,211,131 retrieved papers across 4 disciplines (Cardiology, Neurology, Neuroscience, and Stroke) from 2015 to 2026. Stroke ranks 2nd of 4 disciplines with a composite open-science index of 21/100, 0 points behind Cardiology (22/100). Stroke's open-access rate rose from 53% (2015) to 74% (2026), a 21 pp gain over 11 years. Stroke leads in dataset sharing at 0%. Registration signals remain rare across all disciplines (peak 3.8%), representing the largest structural gap in open-science practice. Among 71,202 open-access full texts audited for a data-availability statement, Neuroscience most often reports the authors' own deposit in a repository (11%). In Stroke, high-impact papers show 0% dataset sharing vs 0% for the full cohort (1.2× higher), suggesting a visibility gradient in data-sharing practice. Positive trends over the most recent 5 years: Open-science composite index (0–100), Signals beyond open access, and Registration signal.
