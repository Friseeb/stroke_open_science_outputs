Open-Science Uptake Across Stroke and Adjacent Fields

This benchmark covers 776,186 retrieved papers across 4 disciplines (Cardiology, Neurology, Neuroscience, and Stroke) from 2015 to 2026. Stroke ranks 3rd of 4 disciplines with a composite open-science index of 12/100, 1 points behind Neuroscience (13/100). Stroke's open-access rate rose from 39% (2015) to 67% (2026), a 28 pp gain over 11 years. Stroke leads in dataset sharing at 2%. Preregistration remains rare across all disciplines (peak 0.0%), representing the largest structural gap in open-science practice. In Stroke, high-impact papers show 0% dataset sharing vs 2% for the full cohort (0.2× higher), suggesting a visibility gradient in data-sharing practice. Positive trends over the most recent 5 years: Open-science composite index (0–100), Signals beyond open access, and Open-access rate.
